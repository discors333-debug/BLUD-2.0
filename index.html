Sandstrider

Desert Survival

Survive as many days as you can in the desert by keeping your body heat, water and fat in balance.

Difficulty

Goal

Each day lasts 90 seconds. Your score is the number of days you survive.

Your four bars

  • Body heat: keep it between the two white marks (33–41°C). The midday sun heats you fast and the night freezes you.
  • Water: drains over time, and faster when you walk or sprint in the heat.
  • Fat (hump): drains slowly. When water is low, your hump turns fat into water.
  • Health: drops while any bar is in the red. At zero, the game is over.

A day in the desert

  • Dawn: dew forms on some rocks. Drink it before it dries up mid-morning.
  • Midday: it reaches about 45°C. Hide in a burrow (25°C) or under an acacia tree (12°C cooler).
  • Afternoon and dusk: forage for beetles, seeds and cactus fruit. Watch the sky for hawks.
  • Night: it drops to about 3°C. Get back into a burrow and hold Space to sleep through it.

Also out there

  • Oasis in the middle of the map: hold Space to drink. It refills every dawn.
  • Golden scarabs are rare and fast, and restore health as well as fat.
  • Hawks circle, then dive at a red ring. Run out of it or duck into a burrow.
  • Sandstorms slow you down but can't hurt you.
  • Weather changes daily: heatwaves, cold snaps and windy days.
  • Evolution: every new dawn, pick one of three traits to make your sandstrider tougher.

Built-in adaptations

  • Radiator ears: your tall ears shed body heat faster when the air cools. Great at dusk, risky at night.
  • Dew spikes: the spikes on your hump channel dew to your mouth for a big water refill.
  • Fat hump: stored fat turns into water when you're running dry.
  • Sealing nostrils: they close in sandstorms so the sand can't hurt you.
  • Wide flat feet: they keep you on top of the soft sand.
